Buying a used phone in Windsor: what to check

Most people buying second-hand worry about the same four things. Here is how we handle each of them, and what to ask any seller — including us.

Battery health

A phone can look perfect and still need charging twice a day. On iPhone the figure lives in Settings › Battery › Battery Health & Charging; anything above 80% is considered healthy by Apple. We show the measured percentage on the listing where we have it, and replace any battery below 80% before the handset goes on sale.

Condition grade, honestly described

Grades vary wildly between sellers, so ours are deliberately plain. Like New means you would not pick it out from a new handset. Excellent means light marks you would struggle to see at arm's length. Good means visible everyday scratches. No cracked screens or dented frames are ever sold as any of these. Every grade is judged on the actual handset, and you are welcome to inspect it in the shop before paying.

Is it really unlocked, and is it clean?

Every phone we sell is unlocked to all UK networks, factory reset, and checked against the national database so it is not reported lost or stolen. Ask any seller for the IMEI and check it yourself — a legitimate seller will hand it over without hesitating.

How long will it keep getting updates?

Software support matters more than raw age. An older iPhone still receiving iOS updates is a safer buy than a newer Android that stopped receiving security patches. Where we know the support window, it is printed on the listing with a link to the manufacturer's own page so you can verify it rather than take our word for it.
