Mail-In Repair FAQs

Is your Windsor shop open?

Not at the moment. Our shop at Queen Annes Court, 3 Peascod Street is temporarily closed due to an issue with the building's lease, so please don't travel in. Repairs carry on by post: book online and we'll email you prepaid postage instructions.

How long does a mail-in repair take?

Most repairs are done within 24 hours of your device arriving, then posted straight back. Parts that have to be ordered in, such as genuine Apple or Samsung service parts, usually take one to three working days, and we tell you before we start.

Is it the same price as the shop?

Yes. The prices in our online booker are the same prices we charge in the shop, and every repair carries the same 12-month warranty on parts and labour.

How do I send my phone?

Book online and we email you secure prepaid postage instructions. Pack it in a small padded box, add a note with your name and booking details, and send it tracked.

How do I pay?

Pay online by card when you book, or choose pay after repair and we'll send you a secure card payment link before we post your device back.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you don't pay for the repair. No fix, no fee, and we'll talk you through your options, including a trade-in on a refurbished phone.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Our technicians only work on the hardware. We never look at your photos, messages or apps, and there's no need to wipe your phone for a screen or battery repair.

Do you repair games consoles by post?

Yes. PS5 and Xbox HDMI port repair is £85, a maintenance clean with fresh thermal paste is £50, and a PS5 liquid metal service is £85, with a free diagnostic and a fixed quote before any work starts.
