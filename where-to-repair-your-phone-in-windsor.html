When the Apple Store Is the Right Trip

There is no Apple Store in Windsor. The nearest is Apple The Oracle in Reading — roughly 40 minutes by car, about an hour by train via Slough. That journey is worth making in three situations: the handset is still in manufacturer warranty, you have AppleCare+, or the fault is something odd that has survived a diagnostic elsewhere.

It is not worth making for a cracked screen or a tired battery on an out-of-warranty phone. You will spend most of a day and pay Apple's published rate for something a Windsor shop will do in half an hour for less. That is not a criticism of Apple — their prices buy genuine parts, trained staff and a warranty backed by the manufacturer. It is just a poor use of a Saturday.

What to Ask Before Handing Over Your Phone

Most of the difference between a good repair and a bad one is settled in the ninety seconds before you hand the device over. Whoever you use, ask these:

  • "Which part are you fitting — genuine, OLED, or LCD?" A shop that cannot answer this crisply is fitting whatever arrived in the last box. On a phone that left the factory with OLED, a cheap LCD is a visible downgrade you should be choosing knowingly, not discovering later.
  • "How long is the warranty, and does it cover the part and the labour?" Get the length and the scope, not just a nod.
  • "What happens if you can't fix it?" No fix, no fee should be the answer. If there is a diagnostic fee, find out now.
  • "Will this show up in Parts and Service History?" iPhones log replaced parts in Settings. A straight answer here tells you a lot about the shop.
  • "Do you need my passcode?" For a screen or battery, no. Do not give it out for routine work.

The parts question is the one that separates shops. Ours is answered in full on our genuine and OEM parts page — including when we think the expensive option is not worth your money.

Repair or Replace?

A repair is usually the cheaper answer when the handset still gets security updates and only one component has failed. The calculation flips when the quote starts approaching what a checked, warrantied refurbished handset of the same model costs — or when water has been through several components, where today's fix can be next month's new fault.

A rough rule that holds up: if the repair costs more than half the price of replacing the handset with an equivalent refurbished one, think hard. We will tell you which side of that line you are on before quoting, and we buy handsets for cash either way, so we do not particularly mind which you choose.

Common Questions

Is there an Apple Store in Windsor?

No. The nearest is Apple The Oracle in Reading — about 40 minutes by car, an hour by train via Slough. Worth it for in-warranty work; rarely worth it for a screen or battery.

How much is a screen repair in Windsor?

Independent shops generally start around £40 for older iPhone models on a budget panel, rising with model and panel quality to roughly £170 for the newest handsets. Genuine manufacturer parts cost more and are ordered in. Chains and manufacturer channels sit higher and mostly do not publish prices.

How long does it take?

Under 30 minutes for a screen the shop has in stock, 30–60 minutes for a battery. Board-level work or an ordered-in genuine part takes days, wherever you go.

Is it safe to leave my phone?

For screen or battery work, yes — no passcode needed, no wipe needed, it is all hardware. If a shop wants your passcode for a screen swap, ask why.

Should I repair or replace?

Repair, if it still gets security updates and one component has failed. Replace, if it is past updates, water has hit several parts, or the quote is close to the price of an equivalent refurbished handset.
