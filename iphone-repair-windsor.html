Repair it, or replace it?

Sometimes a repair is not the right answer. If the handset is several years old, has water damage across the board, or has stopped receiving iOS security updates, the money is often better spent moving on. We will tell you honestly which side of that line your phone falls on — and we do not charge for the diagnostic either way.

If replacing makes more sense, we sell checked used iPhones in Windsor with battery health shown up front and a 3-year warranty, and we will buy your old handset for cash or trade-in to bring the price down. Cracked but otherwise fine? Repair is almost always the cheaper route.

Genuine Parts, or the Sensible Alternative

You should be told what is going into your phone, and you should get to choose it. We fit genuine Apple service parts on request — ordered in, usually one to three working days, quoted before you commit — alongside premium aftermarket OLED, FHD incell and LCD tiers available the same day. We will tell you which one your phone warrants, including when the expensive option is not worth it.
