Why a PS5 Starts Running Hot

Sony cools the PS5's main chip with liquid metal instead of ordinary thermal paste. It carries heat extremely well, but after years of heating and cooling it can shift or leave dry patches on the chip. Once part of the chip loses contact with the heatsink, the fan has to work much harder and the console can overheat.

🌪️ Fan running flat out

The console sounds like a hairdryer within minutes of starting a game.

🌡️ "Your PS5 is too hot"

The on-screen temperature warning appears, often followed by a shutdown.

⏻ Shutdowns in big games

It runs menus and lighter games fine, then powers off in demanding ones.

🧹 Never been opened

A launch-era PS5 has had years to collect dust. That alone raises temperatures.

Questions People Ask

What is the liquid metal in a PS5?

Instead of ordinary thermal paste, Sony uses a liquid metal compound between the PS5's main chip and its heatsink. It moves heat away from the chip very efficiently, which is how the console stays cool under load.

How do I know if my PS5 needs a liquid metal service?

The usual signs are a fan that runs flat out, a 'Your PS5 is too hot' warning, or the console shutting itself down in demanding games. Dust alone can cause the same symptoms, so we check first and tell you which it is.

Does every PS5 need this doing?

No. A PS5 that runs quietly and never overheats doesn't need opening. This service is for consoles that already run hot or loud, and for older consoles being serviced anyway.

Can I do it myself?

It's a risky DIY job. Liquid metal conducts electricity, so a stray drop on the main board can short it out. It also needs the barrier around the chip to be sealed properly afterwards.

Does this work on an Xbox or PS4?

Xbox Series X, Series S and PS4 use conventional thermal paste rather than liquid metal. For those we do a £50 maintenance service: full clean and fresh thermal paste.

Will opening my PS5 affect Sony's warranty?

If your console is still inside Sony's manufacturer warranty and is overheating, speak to Sony first, as a third-party repair may affect that cover. Outside warranty, our own work carries a 12-month warranty.
