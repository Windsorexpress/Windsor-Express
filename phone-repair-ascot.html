Phone Repair FAQs for Ascot

Can I wait while my phone is repaired?

Yes — most screen repairs take under 30 minutes, so grab a coffee on Peascod Street and it’ll be ready when you’re back. Batteries take 30–60 minutes.

Yes — iPhone, Samsung and Android handsets, plus iPads, tablets and games consoles, all with the same 12-month warranty.

Then you pay nothing — no fix, no fee. We can also buy the handset for cash or arrange trade-in credit against a refurbished phone.
