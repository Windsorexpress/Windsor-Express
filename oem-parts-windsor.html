What "OEM" Actually Means

OEM stands for original equipment manufacturer — the part the manufacturer fits on the production line, or supplies through its own service channel. In phone repair the word gets stretched until it means almost nothing, so here is how we use it, plainly:

  • Genuine service part — supplied by Apple or Samsung through their repair channels. New, in manufacturer packaging, with the manufacturer's own part number.
  • Original harvested / OEM-refurbished — a genuine panel pulled from a donor handset and re-laminated onto a new glass front. Genuine panel, refurbished assembly.
  • Premium aftermarket OLED — a third-party OLED made to match the original closely. Not a manufacturer part, but on a two-or-three-year-old handset most people cannot tell without the two side by side.
  • FHD incell / LCD — third-party LCD technology fitted to a phone that left the factory with OLED. Cheapest option, visibly different in direct sunlight and on black backgrounds.

Our position: we will tell you which tier your handset actually warrants, including when the expensive option is a waste of your money. We would rather you came back in two years than paid £80 more today.

Parts Pairing, Warning Messages and What They Mean

Modern iPhones log every replaced part. Open Settings → General → About → Parts and Service History and iOS lists what has been changed. This is worth understanding before any repair, wherever you have it done.

Since Apple's April 2024 change, genuine Apple parts — new or used — are calibrated on the device after fitting rather than needing to be paired in advance, and the Parts and Service History screen shows whether a genuine part is new or used. Apple also extended Activation Lock to parts, so calibration is restricted on components harvested from a device reported lost or stolen. That is a good thing: it is what makes a legitimate harvested-original market possible.

Aftermarket panels and batteries can trigger a service message and may switch off True Tone or battery-health reporting, depending on model and iOS version. None of this stops the phone working. What matters is that you are told before the repair, not when you get the phone back. We tell you.

Source: Apple Newsroom, April 2024 — Apple to expand repair options with support for used genuine parts.

Does This Affect My Manufacturer Warranty?

Straight answer: under UK consumer law a manufacturer cannot void your entire warranty just because somebody else opened the device. What Apple and Samsung will do is decline cover on the part that was replaced, and on any fault they can attribute to it. In practice, if your handset is still inside its manufacturer warranty and the fault is not accidental damage, the manufacturer route is usually the better one — and we will say so rather than take the job.

Outside manufacturer warranty, the cover that matters is ours: 12 months on parts and labour, on every tier, plus no fix no fee if we cannot solve it. See our warranty terms for the detail.

Genuine & OEM Parts FAQs

Can I get a genuine Apple part fitted in Windsor?

Yes — on request, at our Peascod Street shop. Genuine parts are not stocked for every model, so they are ordered in, usually one to three working days, and quoted before you commit. If you would rather not wait, a premium aftermarket OLED is available same day.

What is the difference between an OEM screen and an aftermarket screen?

OEM is the manufacturer's own part. Aftermarket is third-party, in tiers. On an OLED iPhone, genuine or premium aftermarket OLED keeps true blacks and full brightness; LCD and FHD incell do not match the original outdoors or on dark backgrounds, but cost considerably less and carry the same 12-month warranty.

Will my iPhone show an "Unknown Part" message?

iOS logs replaced parts under Settings → General → About → Parts and Service History. Genuine parts calibrate on-device and show as genuine, new or used. Aftermarket parts can show a service message and may disable True Tone or battery health on some models. We tell you which to expect for your model before we start.

Does a non-genuine part void my warranty?

Not your whole warranty — UK law does not allow that. But the manufacturer will normally decline cover on the replaced part and on faults arising from it. If you are still in manufacturer warranty we will tell you to use it.

Is a genuine part always worth the extra?

No. On a two-to-four-year-old handset you are keeping, premium aftermarket OLED is usually the better value. Genuine earns its price on near-new phones, on resale value, and where Face ID or True Tone matter.

Do you fit genuine Samsung parts?

Yes, on request. Samsung supplies most Galaxy displays as a full service pack — panel, frame and battery together — so it costs more than an aftermarket panel swap but comes out factory fresh. We quote both.
