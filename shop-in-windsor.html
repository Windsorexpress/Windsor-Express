Visiting Windsor and Something's Gone Wrong?

This happens more than you'd think. You're two days into a trip, the phone with every photo and booking on it has a dead battery, a broken charger, or a cracked screen from the cobbles outside the Castle. The nearest Apple Store is in Reading, forty minutes away, and you did not come to Windsor to spend a day in Reading.

We're a two-minute walk from the Castle entrance, open seven days, no appointment. Most screen repairs are done in under 30 minutes — long enough for lunch on Peascod Street. If it's just a charger or a power bank you need, that's a thirty-second visit.

We can also post a repaired device on to you if you've moved on before it's ready. Ask at the counter.
