Console Maintenance Service

GTA 6 lands on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on 19 November 2026. Big open-world games work a console's cooling hard for hours at a time, and a console carrying a few years of dust runs hotter, louder and is more likely to shut itself down to protect the chip. A service before a long session is cheaper than a repair after one.

  • Full strip-down of the console
  • Dust and lint cleared from the fan, heatsink fins and vents
  • Fresh thermal paste on the chip, or the liquid metal checked and re-applied on a PS5
  • HDMI, USB and power ports inspected for damage
  • Reassembled and tested before you collect it

Good for any PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One that has got louder or hotter over time, and for any console that's a few years old and has never been opened.

Console Repair FAQs

Do you fix PS5 HDMI ports in Windsor?

Yes. A damaged HDMI port with no picture is one of the most common PS5 repairs we do. It's a micro-soldering job and it comes with a 12-month warranty.

What's included in the console maintenance service?

A full strip-down, dust cleared from the fan, heatsink and vents, fresh thermal paste (or the liquid metal checked and re-applied on a PS5), ports inspected, and the console tested before you collect it.

Do you service the liquid metal on a PS5?

Yes. If a PS5 is overheating, loud or shutting down, we clean and re-apply the liquid metal on the chip and re-seal it, alongside a full dust clean.

Can you fix Nintendo Switch Joy-Con drift?

Yes, we replace drifting analogue sticks on Joy-Cons and Pro controllers, usually the same day. We also repair Switch screens, batteries and charging ports.

How much does a console repair cost?

The diagnostic is free. We tell you exactly what's wrong and quote a fixed price before any work starts. If we can't fix it, you pay nothing.

How long does a console repair take?

It depends on the fault and on parts, so we give you a turnaround time with your quote when you drop the console in.

Will I lose my saved games?

For hardware repairs like HDMI ports, fans, cleaning and drives, your storage isn't touched. Saves and accounts stay exactly as they were.
